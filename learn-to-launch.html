My Story

From Process Worker to Founder

I left my job to build Wise King Enterprise full-time. No business degree, no investors, no team — just my wife Vanisha and me, figuring it out one form, one supplier, and one late night at a time.

What I did have was a willingness to actually sit down and learn the system — council food registration, HACCP sourcing, GS1 barcoding, FSANZ labelling rules, and eventually a full online store with a real shopping cart and checkout, built without hiring a developer.

I leaned on AI tools heavily to do this — not as a shortcut around the hard parts, but as a way to move fast on the parts that would normally take months or cost thousands in agency fees. Every piece of paperwork, every website change, every product page — I did it myself, with AI doing the heavy lifting on execution while I made the actual business decisions.

If you've got an idea sitting in your head and no idea how to turn it into something real, that's exactly the gap I can help close.
