The Royal Decree

What Makes Each Blend Worthy

The 60-Second Ritual
If your Golden Latte completely dissolves, you aren’t drinking real spices — you are drinking chemical extracts. Because Wise King uses 100% raw, unadulterated botanicals, they naturally settle after a minute or two. This is not a flaw, it is a feature. Those 60 seconds are when the heat of the milk unlocks the active curcumin in our turmeric and draws out the raw power of pure saffron threads. Give your cup a slow, gentle swirl as you drink — an authentic, unfiltered ritual.
Saffron Inside — Rarer Than Gold
Most commercial brands use cheap saffron powder or synthetic flavouring to cut costs. We don’t take shortcuts. Look closely and you’ll see the physical, deep-crimson threads of real saffron — the world’s most expensive spice, hand-harvested from the heart of the Crocus flower. We leave the threads whole so you can witness the raw luxury swirling in your cup, infusing every sip with gold.
The Queen of Spices Meets the Double-Benefit Catalyst
Our recipe is biologically optimised, not just delicious. At its heart is vibrant turmeric, paired deliberately with cracked black pepper — because a pinch of black pepper multiplies your body’s ability to absorb turmeric’s benefits by up to 2,000%. We wrap this golden catalyst in the aromatic luxury of green cardamom, the undisputed Queen of Spices, for a sweet, warm depth without a single gram of sugar.
No Shortcuts, No Fillers
Six hand-selected botanicals. Nothing else. No maltodextrin, no anti-caking agents, no artificial colour to fake the gold you should be tasting. Every gram of every batch is weighed, blended, and packed by hand in Geelong — to a standard that simply does not bend.
Twelve Spices, Zero Compromise
Most spice rubs are mostly salt and filler, with one or two real spices doing the talking. Curry Coast Rub is the opposite: twelve whole spices, hand-balanced in even measure, with no salt added at all. Every spice earns its place — there is no padding to cut costs, only flavour built layer by layer like a coastal sunset.
The Twelve Apostles Standard
Named for the Twelve Apostles on the Great Ocean Road — twelve limestone stacks, each one essential to the view, none replaceable. Our rub follows the same principle: twelve spices, each one essential. Remove one and the whole blend loses its shape. That is the discipline behind every batch.
Rest Before You Cook
Real spice rubs need time to work. Rub generously into meat or vegetables, then rest for 10 minutes before grilling or roasting. In that resting time, the smoked paprika, Kashmiri chilli, and warm cardamom begin to draw moisture and bind to the surface — the difference between a coating and a crust.
Built for the Whole Kitchen
This is not a one-trick blend. Curry Coast Rub works as a dry rub on the grill, stirred through a slow-cooked curry, or folded into a gravy. Twelve premium spices, hand-blended in Geelong, with no salt and no fillers — built to earn a permanent place in your spice drawer.
Two Ingredients. No Argument.
Most café mocha mixes are mostly sugar, with coffee and cocoa as an afterthought. Ours is the reverse: 50% premium instant coffee, 50% dark Dutch-process cocoa. Nothing else. No sugar, no creamer powder, no flavour fillers pretending to be chocolate. What you taste is what we put in — nothing more, nothing less.
The Paste-First Method
Real cocoa and real instant coffee don’t dissolve instantly into cold milk the way sugar-laden mixes do. That’s why the ritual matters: a splash of hot water first, stirred into a smooth paste, fully unlocks the dark cocoa before the milk goes in. Skip this step and you’ll lose half the depth this blend is capable of.
Dutch-Process, Deliberately
We chose Dutch-process cocoa specifically for its smoother, less acidic, deeper flavour compared to natural cocoa powder. Paired with a premium instant coffee rather than a cheap filler blend, the result is closer to a café-bought mocha than anything you’ll find in a supermarket sachet.
Hot or Iced — Built for Both
This blend was designed to work two ways: a rich, warming mocha in winter, or stirred into cold milk and poured over ice in summer. One blend, year-round — sweetened only as much as you choose, never by us.
The Friction Test
Don’t judge us by our cover — judge us by your fingertips. Rub a pinch of our matcha between your fingers. It will completely disappear into your skin like silk, leaving no grain behind. That is the texture of true stone-milled luxury, and it is the fastest way to tell real ceremonial matcha from the rest.
Slow Craft & Scarcity
A single tin takes one hour to make. Our leaves are shade-grown for 20+ days to maximise L-theanine — the calm-focus amino acid — then slowly ground on granite wheels at a rate of just 30g per hour. Every tin you receive represents an hour of dedicated, heat-free milling to preserve the vibrant emerald hue and rich umami flavour.
The Raw Luxury Angle
We put 100% of your money into the leaves, not the landfill. Flashy tins and over-engineered packaging are often used to mask mediocre tea. We chose minimal, low-profile packaging so every dollar goes directly toward sourcing the rarest, purest shade-grown tencha leaves in Japan.
The No-Bitterness Guarantee
If you need sugar, it isn’t our matcha. Pure luxury grade carries zero bitterness. It whisks into a vibrant, creamy froth with a naturally sweet, oceanic finish — designed to be enjoyed traditionally, with just water, a whisk, and a moment of calm.
