✦ Preparation

How to Prepare

Select your product to see the preparation guide.

☀️ Golden Latte — Hot

  1. Add 4g (one level teaspoon) to your cup.
  2. Add 1 tbsp hot water. Stir into a smooth paste — this step is essential.
  3. Pour 230ml hot milk over the paste. Froth 15 seconds.
  4. Add honey or maple syrup to taste.
  5. Enjoy immediately.

🧊 Golden Latte — Iced

  1. Add 4g to your cup.
  2. Add 1 tbsp hot water. Stir into a paste first — essential step.
  3. Pour 230ml cold milk and ice over the paste.
  4. Add sweetener if desired. Stir well.
  5. Enjoy immediately.

🍵 Matcha Latte — Hot

  1. Add 4g to a small bowl or cup.
  2. Add 2 tbsp hot water. Whisk vigorously into a smooth paste.
  3. Pour 230ml hot oat or dairy milk over the paste. Froth.
  4. Sweeten with honey if desired.

🧊 Iced Matcha Latte

  1. Whisk 4g with 2 tbsp hot water into paste.
  2. Pour over a glass of ice and cold milk.
  3. Shake or stir well.
  4. Add sweetener if desired.

☕ Café Mocha — Hot

  1. Add 6g (one heaped teaspoon) to your cup.
  2. Add 2 tbsp hot water. Stir until fully dissolved.
  3. Pour 200ml hot milk over. Froth 15 seconds.
  4. Sweeten if desired.

🧊 Iced Mocha

  1. Dissolve 6g in 2 tbsp hot water.
  2. Pour over a glass of ice and cold milk.
  3. Stir well. Sweeten if desired.
  4. Enjoy immediately.

🥩 As a Dry Rub

  1. Press 6g per 500g meat — chicken, lamb, beef or fish.
  2. Rest 10–30 minutes before cooking.
  3. Grill, roast or pan-fry as usual.
  4. No extra salt needed — 12 spices do the work.

🍛 In Curries & Soups

  1. Add 1–2 tsp to your base while cooking.
  2. Works in rice, lentil dishes, soups and stews.
  3. Mix with olive oil and lemon for a marinade.
  4. Stir into yoghurt for a dipping sauce.
