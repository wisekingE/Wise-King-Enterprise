The Royal Decree

What Makes Each Blend Worthy

The 60-Second Ritual
If your Golden Latte completely dissolves, you aren’t drinking real spices — you are drinking chemical extracts. Because Wise King uses 100% raw, unadulterated botanicals, they naturally settle after a minute or two. This is not a flaw, it is a feature. Those 60 seconds are when the heat of the milk unlocks the active curcumin and draws out the raw power of pure saffron threads.
Saffron Inside — Rarer Than Gold
Look closely and you’ll see the physical, deep-crimson threads of real saffron — the world’s most expensive spice, hand-harvested from the heart of the Crocus flower. We leave the threads whole so you can witness the raw luxury swirling in your cup, infusing every sip with gold.
The Queen of Spices Meets the Double-Benefit Catalyst
At its heart is vibrant turmeric, paired deliberately with cracked black pepper — because a pinch of black pepper multiplies your body’s ability to absorb turmeric’s benefits by up to 2,000%. We wrap this in the aromatic luxury of green cardamom for a sweet, warm depth without a single gram of sugar.
No Shortcuts, No Fillers
Six hand-selected botanicals. Nothing else. No maltodextrin, no anti-caking agents, no artificial colour to fake the gold you should be tasting. Every gram of every batch is weighed, blended, and packed entirely by hand in Geelong — to a standard that simply does not bend.
Twelve Spices, Zero Compromise
Most spice rubs are mostly salt and filler. Curry Coast Rub is the opposite: twelve whole spices, hand-balanced in even measure, with no salt added at all. Every spice earns its place — there is no padding, only flavour built layer by layer.
The Twelve Apostles Standard
Named for the Twelve Apostles on the Great Ocean Road — twelve limestone stacks, each one essential, none replaceable. Our rub follows the same principle: twelve spices, each one essential. Remove one and the whole blend loses its shape.
Rest Before You Cook
Rub generously into meat or vegetables, then rest for 10 minutes before grilling or roasting. In that resting time, the smoked paprika, Kashmiri chilli, and warm cardamom begin to bind to the surface — the difference between a coating and a crust.
Built for the Whole Kitchen
This is not a one-trick blend. Curry Coast Rub works as a dry rub on the grill, stirred through a slow-cooked curry, or folded into a gravy. Twelve premium spices, hand-blended in Geelong, with no salt and no fillers — built to earn a permanent place in your spice drawer.
Two Ingredients. No Argument.
Most café mocha mixes are mostly sugar. Ours is the reverse: 50% premium instant coffee, 50% dark Dutch-process cocoa. Nothing else. No sugar, no creamer powder, no flavour fillers pretending to be chocolate.
The Paste-First Method
Real cocoa and real instant coffee don’t dissolve instantly into cold milk. That’s why the ritual matters: a splash of hot water first, stirred into a smooth paste, fully unlocks the dark cocoa before the milk goes in.
Dutch-Process, Deliberately
We chose Dutch-process cocoa specifically for its smoother, less acidic, deeper flavour. Paired with a premium instant coffee, the result is closer to a café-bought mocha than anything you’ll find in a supermarket sachet.
Hot or Iced — Built for Both
A rich, warming mocha in winter, or stirred into cold milk and poured over ice in summer. One blend, year-round — sweetened only as much as you choose, never by us.
The Friction Test
Rub a pinch of our matcha between your fingers. It will completely disappear into your skin like silk, leaving no grain behind. That is the texture of true stone-milled luxury, and it is the fastest way to tell real ceremonial matcha from the rest.
Slow Craft & Scarcity
Our leaves are shade-grown for 20+ days to maximise L-theanine — the calm-focus amino acid — then slowly ground on granite wheels at a rate of just 30g per hour. Every pouch represents an hour of dedicated, heat-free milling.
The Raw Luxury Angle
We put 100% of your money into the leaves, not the landfill. We chose minimal packaging so every dollar goes directly toward sourcing the rarest, purest shade-grown tencha leaves in Japan.
The No-Bitterness Guarantee
If you need sugar, it isn’t our matcha. Pure luxury grade carries zero bitterness. It whisks into a vibrant, creamy froth with a naturally sweet, oceanic finish.
The Friction Test
Rub a pinch of our matcha between your fingers. It will completely disappear into your skin like silk, leaving no grain behind. That is the texture of true stone-milled luxury, and it is the fastest way to tell real ceremonial matcha from the rest. Most powders leave grit. Ours leaves nothing but vivid green.
Uji, Kyoto — 350 Years of Mastery
Our matcha comes from Yamasan Co., Uji City, Kyoto — the most prestigious matcha-growing region in Japan, with over 800 years of cultivation history. Produced in collaboration with local tea masters on a 350-year-old protected tea plantation, blessed with the ideal climate, soil, and altitude for ceremonial-grade tea. Not just Japanese matcha. Uji matcha — the origin that defines the standard.
Lab-Tested. Certified Clean.
Every batch is backed by a Certificate of Analysis from Yamasan's Overseas Division. Results: Heavy metals — not detected. Pesticide residue — not detected. E. coli — negative. Coliform — negative. Tested under Japanese Pharmaceutical (JP) Regulation standards. You are not taking our word for it — you are reading the lab results. The paperwork exists and is held on file.
JAS Organic Certified — Independently Inspected
Yamasan holds JAS Organic Certification (No. 17C-004) issued by the Hyogo Prefectural Organic Agriculture Society (HOAS), certified since 2018 and annually inspected — most recently November 2023. JAS is Japan's national organic agricultural standard, requiring rigorous third-party auditing of the entire growing and production process. Vegan. Gluten free. 100% pure — nothing added.

How to Prepare

I
Add 4g (one level teaspoon) to your cup.
II
Add a splash of hot water. Stir into a smooth paste — this step is essential.
III
Pour 230ml warm or frothed milk over the paste.
IV
Sweeten with honey or maple syrup to taste. Enjoy immediately.
I
Use 6g per 250g of meat or vegetables.
II
Rub evenly into the surface. Rest for 10 minutes before cooking.
III
Grill, roast, or pan-sear as desired.
IV
Also excellent stirred into curries or gravies — adjust to taste.
I
Add 6g (one heaped teaspoon) to a mug.
II
Add a splash of hot water. Stir into a smooth paste.
III
Pour in 200ml warm or frothed milk.
IV
Sweeten to taste. Also delicious served over ice.
I
Sift 1.5g into a bowl to remove any clumps.
II
Add 70–80°C water — not boiling. Whisk briskly in a ‘W’ motion until frothy.
III
For a latte: add a splash of hot water, whisk to a paste, then add 200ml warm frothed milk.
IV
Sweeten with honey if desired.
I
Measure 15g (3 tsp) for 250g vegetables or paneer. Use 18g for 250g meat.
II
Add 3 tablespoons of warm water. Mix into a smooth, thick paste — let it sit 1 minute so the powders fully hydrate.
III
Heat 2 tablespoons of oil or ghee in a pan on medium-high. Add the paste — it will sizzle. Stir constantly for 2 full minutes. This is the bhuna step — do not skip it.
IV
Add your protein or vegetables. Pour in 150ml water. Salt to taste. Cover and simmer 6–8 minutes. Garnish with fresh coriander.
