How to Prepare

Two ways to experience your golden latte — warm and comforting, or cool and refreshing.

☀️

Golden Latte

  1. Whisk together 6 g of golden powder and 20 mL of hot (not boiling) water.
  2. Add 230 mL of hot milk of your choice.
  3. Add sweetener if desired.
  4. Stir well and enjoy!
🧊

Iced Golden Latte

  1. Whisk together 6 g of golden powder and 20 mL of hot (not boiling) water.
  2. Add 230 mL of cold milk of your choice and ice cubes.
  3. Add sweetener if desired.
  4. Stir well and enjoy!
